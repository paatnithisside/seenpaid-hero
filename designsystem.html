Token markers

Most of the hero’s values are written inline in its CSS. Only 19 are variables today.

Variable
A custom property in the hero’s :root, used as written.
Literal
A value written inline in the hero’s CSS. The name is a proposal so it can be promoted to a variable.

Rules the hero holds to

Flat canvas
The page is --canvas. No dot pattern, gradient or texture sits behind the composition.
One accent
Deep navy --blue is the only accent. Green appears only for verified states. Gold belongs to the coin.
Light boards
All three boards use the light theme, Results included.
Authored lines
The headline is exactly three lines. The body copy is three lines with the shortest last. Neither reflows on desktop.
Copy first
The copy reveals while the boards sit in a skeleton loading state. They resolve one by one only after the body copy has appeared.
Alive, not loud
After the intro the pipeline keeps streaming and the cursor keeps wandering. Reduced motion shows the finished frame and nothing moves.
Honest data
Figures are sample data and labelled so. The unmatched payment is shown and marked as not counted.

Open items

Nav and secondary link
Product, Platforms, Pricing, Resources and “See how it works” point at placeholder anchors.
Real case study
The review’s $139 single-post example is not used. It needs real click and sale counts.
Token promotion
The literals on these screens are not variables yet. Promoting them is a mechanical change to the hero.
